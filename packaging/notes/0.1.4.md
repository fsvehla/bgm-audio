## What's new

- **Open audio files from Finder:** Open With → Ambience, or drop a file on the Dock icon. Ambience does not become your default player.
- **Window menu:** Minimize (⌘M), Zoom, and Mini Player (⌘⇧M).
- The mini player toggle uses proper chevron icons.
- Long subtitles are shortened in the middle instead of being cut off.
- The window can no longer be made so narrow that the controls overlap.
- Updated UI framework (GPUI and gpui-component).
